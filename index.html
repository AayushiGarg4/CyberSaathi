Common Cyber Threats

Phishing Attacks

Fake emails or websites that trick you into handing over your passwords or personal info.

CRITICAL

Ransomware

Malware that locks your files and demands payment to give them back.

CRITICAL

Social Engineering

Manipulating people psychologically to reveal confidential information.

HIGH

Data Breaches

Hackers steal personal or corporate data, often selling it on the dark web.

HIGH

Man-in-the-Middle

Attackers secretly intercept your internet traffic to steal data.

MEDIUM

Credential Stuffing

Bots try leaked passwords from old breaches on your other accounts.
